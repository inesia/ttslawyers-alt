Vision & Legal Reform

VISION, HERITAGE STATEMENT AND REFORM OF WORLD LAW
REVOLUTIONARY LEGAL REFORM: FROM "UNSECURED CREDITOR" TO "GENERAL CREDITOR"
Singapore can now be said to be one of most advanced countries in Asia. Founder studied the Insolvency, Restructuring and Dissolution Act (IRDA) as comparative study. IRDA stipulates: “If a company does not have enough funds to pay off its unsecured creditors, the debts owed to them may be reduced in equal proportions, or left unpaid entirely”. Founder delivers constructive criticism of IRDA which disadvantages certain creditor.

“The substance of bankruptcy is so simple" - merely business legal relationship between Creditors and Debtor in the form of money, goods and/or services, therefore business should multiply, be mutually beneficial, and grow larger, safe in protection of law. But what happens is contrary - certain business actors as creditors can fall into poverty due to bankruptcy, because Bankruptcy Law was designed by experts worldwide with possibility of infiltration by cunning free-riders, making it difficult and complicated.

With Law, Your Business Is Secured, therefore, the Founder holds a Revolutionary View: From all Books of Bankruptcy Law and Law Dictionaries in the world, the term "Unsecured Creditor(s)" should be REVOLUTIONARILY ABOLISHED and replaced with "General Creditor(s)" - because a legal battle fought with strong reasoning must be won. This is consistent with the Founder's Bankruptcy Law Theory, based on determination and philosophy.
Founder contributed thoughts to Cross-Border Insolvency-Model Law and to World Bank as Open Knowledge Repository led by Mr. Ajay Banga, Indian diaspora, American national. Founder's view is "precise and correct" that Bankruptcy Laws around world benefit certain creditors while disadvantaging others. This is a postulate.

Statement and Aspiration becoming "Heritage Statement": By Grace of God Almighty, at right time, Founder or succeeding generation will be pioneer at forefront, standing alongside President of Republic of Indonesia delivering speech before UN General Assembly conveying "Civilized Law" - universalism and uniformity of bankruptcy law in world that must protect all creditors. There are business transactions worldwide physically and/or digitally, therefore reasonable: "One World - One Bankruptcy Law" for civilized nations, for justice and prosperity of mankind for generations to come.
