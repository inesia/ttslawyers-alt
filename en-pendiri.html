EXECUTIVE FIRM PROFILE

Dr. (Commerce) Timotius Tumbur Simbolon, SH., MH. – “Timoti Sim” - Founder & Managing Partner

Prior to founding this Law Firm, he served as Head of Legal of a Multinational Corporation – World Class whose majority shareholder is a Swiss corporation. For 5 years, entrusted as Head of Legal of the Indonesia - China Business Council (ICBC), an association of Indonesian entrepreneurs conducting business and investing reciprocally with Chinese entrepreneurs and the Government of China. Within the Advocates Organization, the Indonesian Advocates Association (AAI), the Founder previously served as Head of Department of Foreign Affairs.

The Founder possesses extensive expertise in Banking, National and International Arbitration, and specifically in National and International Bankruptcy (Cross-Border Insolvency - Model Law), Receiver and member of INSOL - a rare qualification in Indonesia. This aligns with his Doctoral Thesis: “The Balance Of Creditors' Rights In Bankruptcy” with comparative studies in advanced jurisdictions, whose discussion continues to resonate in the world.

As Legal Counsel, Founder provides legal advice that is “accurate and robust” to protect clients' legal interests, business continuity, and future success.

When disputes arise from agreements, this Law Firm, with its extensive experience and “Fair Trial”, we fight the most difficult legal battles on legal merit based on high-quality legal knowledge with substantial financial benefit value for client.

Landmark Matters Won Under Founder's Counsel:

  • Representing State-Owned Bank (BUMN) vs Debtors - all successfully handled.
  • Major textile company in Central Java vs large European foreign bank in Indonesia.
  • National Arbitration of a large Group Company vs State Owned Enterprise.
  • International Arbitration in Singapore (SIAC) between State Owned Mining Company of Republic of Indonesia vs State Owned Chinese Companies. The settlement was in China.
  • Most notably, what can only be described as “spectacular” out-of-court settlements between a major American Geothermal Company vs Indonesian Company, and the settlement of a Largest Dairy Milk Industry - both very fast and highly satisfactory.

The Founder authored several books distinct from general bankruptcy books, with constructive criticism to world-renowned Commerce Law and Bankruptcy Law experts from America: Honorable Prof. Elizabeth Warren (US Presidential Candidate 2020), Prof. Jay Lawrence Westbrook (World Bank Advisor), but full support to Prof. Kenneth Cork and Prof. Vanessa Finch from the United Kingdom on the meaning of Creditor Rights Reform.

He advised the government on harmonization of Bankruptcy Law at least in ASEAN Countries as reciprocity principle, quoting the President of Republic of Indonesia at ASEAN Summit: “At least we need legal harmonization in a certain region.”
